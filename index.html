Abstract

Railway power systems have improved by magnitudes since the first electric power systems in the 19th century. This paper analyses the historical development of railway power systems and evaluates how technology has influenced efficiency, reliability and sustainability. This paper investigates the transitions from dc to ac systems, alongside modern traction converters and electrical motors. Regenerative braking and energy storage technologies are also explored. The British railway network provides context to the coexistence of ac overhead and dc third rail electrification respectively.
